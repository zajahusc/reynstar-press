Poetry

Inevitable Constellations

A collection of poetry about the people we love, the lives we imagine, and the strange inevitability of becoming who we are. Inevitable Constellations traces intimacy, loss, longing, and memory through the quiet connections that only become visible when we look backward—like stars that were always there, waiting for us to draw the lines between them.

By Zac James Published June 2025

Fiction

The End

The flare arrives without warning. In the aftermath, the world does not end all at once—it empties. Years later, Elliot has built something resembling a life in the ruins, but survival has a way of preserving the things we thought the end of the world would erase. A novel about friendship, grief, isolation, and what remains when the future everyone expected simply stops coming.

Queer Literary Fiction / Romance

Chasing Stars

Running from a life he no longer recognizes, a man finds himself drawn into a love affair as beautiful as it is impossible to hold onto. Chasing Stars is a queer love story about desire, depression, and the people we mistake for destinations—and whether some people enter our lives simply to teach us how to keep moving after they are gone.

By Zac James Published August 2026

The house

We publish the books that ask for time.

Reynstar Press exists on a simple premise: publish work that is genuinely excellent, and let nothing else decide what gets made.

We are not chasing trend cycles or what the market says will sell. We are committed to good writing, bold ideas, and a publishing culture that is queer-friendly and unhurried—built for readers who want more than what sells.

Serious intentions. A long life for the work.

“We believe a great book should feel as considered in its design as it is in its language.”
