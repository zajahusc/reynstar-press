The house

We publish the books that ask for time.

Reynstar Press is a new independent press built around a simple idea: publish work that deserves a wider readership—books by underrepresented writers, overlooked communities, and voices shaped by depth rather than market pressure.

We are starting small, staying rigorous, and building a list around literary excellence over trend cycles. We are committed to good writing, bold ideas, and a publishing culture that is queer-friendly, anti-extractive, and open to readers who want more than what sells.

New press. Serious intentions. A long life for the work.

“We believe a great book should feel as considered in its design as it is in its language.”
