We publish the books that ask for time.

Reynstar Press is an independent publisher of literary fiction, poetry, and genre-blending works exploring identity, connection, memory, and the complicated ways we search for meaning. Based in Chicago, Reynstar Press publishes emotionally resonant work that embraces unconventional storytelling and the spaces between realism and the imagined.

About Zac James

Zac James is a Chicago-based writer whose work explores love, identity, memory, and the strange places we go searching for ourselves. His writing blends queer experience with existential themes, often drawing inspiration from travel, mythology, nature, and the complicated ways people find and lose one another.
