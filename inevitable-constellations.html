Poetry

Inevitable Constellations

By zac james

Publication: September 1, 2026

Inevitable Constellations is an intimate journey through memory, love, trauma, identity, and the search for meaning in an impossibly vast universe. Moving from the fever-dream forests of childhood to distant planets, ruined worlds, dark oceans, and the infinite expanse of the stars, zac james transforms the deeply personal into something cosmic. These poems trace a life spent learning to escape our given roles, love without certainty, survive betrayal, make peace with the past, and confront the terrifying beauty of our own impermanence.

Inevitable Constellations asks what remains when everything we know eventually disappears. Its answer is both simple and infinite: love. The people we hold, the lives we touch, and the pieces of ourselves we leave behind become points of light in something larger than any one lifetime. We are temporary, but perhaps nothing about us is truly lost. We are stardust finding its way home.
