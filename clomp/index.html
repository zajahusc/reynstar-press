Clomp: The Nihilistic Dinosaur

By Zac R. James

Publication: November 3, 2026

Clomp is a nihilist with a tidy stack of stone tablets and a long list of things the other dinosaurs would rather ignore. They hoard food, fight over whose roar matters most, and argue while a baby triceratops cries.

Then a meteor appears in the sky. As the valley panics, Clomp calmly records what he has seen—and tells the others what they could have done differently. Darkly funny and pointed, Clomp: The Nihilistic Dinosaur is a children’s story about greed, kindness, and caring for the world we share.
