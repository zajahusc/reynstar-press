The House

A place for new voices.

Reynstar Press is an independent publishing house based in Chicago, specializing in online publishing and giving new authors a platform to share their work.

We publish literary fiction, poetry, and genre-blending work that explores identity, connection, memory, and the complicated ways we search for meaning. We are drawn to emotionally resonant writing, unconventional storytelling, and the spaces between realism and the imagined.

Publishing for a connected world

Online publishing is central to how we bring books and readers together. We see the internet as a place to discover a new voice, build a relationship with an author’s work, and make independent literature part of everyday life. Our aim is to give each book a considered home online, with room for its story, its author, and the readers who will carry it forward.

A platform for new authors

A writer’s first opportunity can make a lasting difference. Reynstar Press exists to create space for emerging authors and perspectives that deserve to be heard. We want to help new voices reach readers while preserving the individuality that makes their work worth sharing.

We value curiosity, care, and a willingness to take creative risks. Whether a work begins with a quiet observation or an imagined world, we look for language and ideas that stay with us long after the last page.
